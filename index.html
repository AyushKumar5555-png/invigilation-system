Help & Info

About BIT MESRA Allocation Engine

This is a modern light-mode interface powered by a hybrid **Greedy**, **Hill-Climbing Local Search**, and **Branch-and-Bound** scheduler written in Python.

How to allocate invigilation duties:

  1. Click **Run Allocation** to request a schedule.
  2. Inspect workload indicators (e.g. Jain's Index).
  3. Configure staff lists and availability constraints by opening the **Faculty List** or **Sessions Config** panels.
  4. Click **Export Config** to download the active dataset.

Ratio Modes:

  • Target Load Scaling: Targets are scaled proportionally to meet invigilation hours demands.
  • Raw Weights: Ratio weights represent target hours directly.
  • Hard Category Limits: Targets are treated as hard maximum bounds.
